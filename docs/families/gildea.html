Gildea

A timeline of the records that name this townland.

Each event comes from one record and is shown as that record wrote it. Names are spelled as written. A record is placed here only when the source ties it to the townland, so gaps in the timeline are gaps in the surviving records, not in the life of the place.

    Baptism records come from our own transcription of the register images held by the National Library of Ireland.
